# 画面设置

#### **纹理显存&#x20;**<mark style="color:blue;">**高**</mark>

<figure><img src="https://2250278911-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FSwotjVkQd1faB6N1f6j4%2Fuploads%2Fuien1rohiGPguAASJF7y%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=d7bfd1d0-6a91-4208-a4da-01478e56cf12" alt=""><figcaption><p>务必保持为<strong>高</strong></p></figcaption></figure>

#### **体积光&#x20;**<mark style="color:$danger;">**关闭**</mark>**&#x20;& 体积雾质量&#x20;**<mark style="color:$danger;">**禁用**</mark>

<figure><img src="https://2250278911-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FSwotjVkQd1faB6N1f6j4%2Fuploads%2Fy5TVB7vfsD4oHWK6BSOS%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=75cd24a0-0fa9-4ad2-bcff-fd7b789d315c" alt=""><figcaption><p><strong>移除雾气</strong></p></figcaption></figure>

#### **粒子质量：低 & GPU 粒子质量&#x20;**<mark style="color:$danger;">**禁用**</mark>

<figure><img src="https://2250278911-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FSwotjVkQd1faB6N1f6j4%2Fuploads%2FLy1D832BDIPK2lTNlSkM%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=b8179756-36d7-446a-9720-27140c334907" alt=""><figcaption><p><strong>粒子效果容易干扰视线，关闭后可减少碎屑特效</strong></p></figcaption></figure>

#### **扭曲&#x20;**<mark style="color:$danger;">**关闭**</mark>

<figure><img src="https://2250278911-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FSwotjVkQd1faB6N1f6j4%2Fuploads%2FBXCXQtSPNJylg1PvK9vI%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=e1aa27a5-6496-4511-90af-da7118819459" alt=""><figcaption><p><strong>便于在远处发现兆力使</strong></p></figcaption></figure>

#### **色彩校正&#x20;**<mark style="color:$danger;">**关闭**</mark>

<figure><img src="https://2250278911-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FSwotjVkQd1faB6N1f6j4%2Fuploads%2FDKAu1Rq1YE1AenZW0TCj%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=9bd407b4-97c5-4350-abe1-c43b20e088d7" alt=""><figcaption><p><strong>此项很重要，可以避免强光晃眼</strong></p></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
如果你是冲击排行榜的**竞速玩家**，建议**关闭其余画面特效**，尽量保持帧率稳定。

也可以使用 **DLSS** 的**性能**或**超级性能**模式进行画面放大，在维持观感的同时提高性能。
{% endhint %}
